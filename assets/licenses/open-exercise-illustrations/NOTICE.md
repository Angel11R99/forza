# Open Exercise Illustrations — attribution and provenance

The start/end frame assets packaged under `assets/exercise_animations/cc0-*`
come from [Marvin Nazari's Open Exercise Illustrations repository](https://github.com/MarvinNazari/exercise-images), pinned to commit `2786aefa4b36a08faeca8114e0d1eb0af3ef6732`.

The source repository declares the artwork CC0 1.0 Universal. Its full license text is preserved in `LICENSE`; the source also requests an appreciated link back and supplies the images without warranty. The source warns that the illustrations are AI-generated, have not been reviewed by a qualified coach or medical professional, and may contain imperfect details. FORZA uses these only for exact movement matches and labels all exercise media as illustrative.
